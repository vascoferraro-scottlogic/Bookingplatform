The club's user list lives at **Users** in the sidebar.

## Roles within a club

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| `USER` | Make bookings, message in channels, view events |
| `MAINTENANCE` | Everything a `USER` can, plus see and update maintenance tasks |
| `TENANT_ADMIN` | Full club admin — bookings, content, events, users, settings |

`PLATFORM_ADMIN` is a separate role that lives outside any single club.

## Inviting

1. Click **Invite member** on the Users page.
2. Enter their email; an invitation email is sent with a sign-up link.
3. The new account starts as `USER`. Change the role from the user's row when they need it.

## Suspending

Suspended users can no longer sign in but their booking history is preserved. Use suspension instead of deletion when in doubt — deletion is irreversible and cascades through audit attribution.
