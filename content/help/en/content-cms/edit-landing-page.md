Your public landing page is composed of section blocks: **Hero**, **About**, **Photo**, **Map** and **Contact**. Each block has its own draft → review → published → archived lifecycle.

## Editing a section

1. Open **Content** in the sidebar.
2. Click an existing section to edit, or **Create section**.
3. Each section type has its own JSON shape — the editor pre-fills a template you can fill in.

## The workflow

- **Draft** — only visible to admins; safe to edit freely.
- **Review** — submitted for review; another admin can publish or send back to draft.
- **Published** — live on the public landing page.
- **Archived** — hidden but preserved; can be re-drafted later.

Use **Disabled** to hide a published section temporarily without losing its content.
