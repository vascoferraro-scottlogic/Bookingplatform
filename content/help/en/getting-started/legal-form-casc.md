**Community Amateur Sports Club (CASC)** isn't a separate legal form — it's a
**tax status** registered with HMRC. You sit on top of an existing structure
(usually an unincorporated association or limited company) and apply for CASC
status to unlock the reliefs.

## Why a club might register as a CASC

- **Mandatory 80% rates relief** on the clubhouse and grounds.
- **Gift Aid** on qualifying donations from members and supporters.
- **Corporate Gift Aid** — companies can deduct donations from corporation tax.
- **Capital gains tax exemption** on sales of club assets.
- **Inheritance tax exemption** on legacies to the club.

## CASC vs. registered charity — which is right?

| | CASC | Registered charity (CIO/SCIO/etc) |
|---|---|---|
| Regulator | HMRC | Charity Commission / OSCR / CCNI |
| Public benefit reporting | Light | Required annually |
| Charitable trust funding | Limited | Full access |
| Trading restrictions | Looser | Tighter |
| Bar/social activities | Allowed within limits | Trickier |
| Membership fees | Cap of £1,612/year (2026) | No formal cap |

**Rule of thumb**: if you mostly want tax efficiency and to keep running like
a club, CASC is simpler. If you need to access the full charity grants
landscape and want trustees to have limited liability, go CIO/SCIO.

## CASC eligibility (the gotchas)

- **Open to the whole community** — no unreasonable joining restrictions.
- **Organised on amateur basis** — pros allowed but the club must be
  fundamentally amateur.
- **Sole purpose is sport** (and ancillary social activities).
- **Annual member income cap** — checked yearly.

## Funding routes a CASC unlocks

- **Sport England / sportscotland** — most programmes accept CASCs.
- **Local authority grants** — varies, but CASCs are often eligible.
- **Brewery / bar industry sponsorships** — non-charitable status keeps these
  cleaner than for a charity.
- **Member donations under Gift Aid** — 25p uplift per qualifying £1.

## Application

Register via HMRC's CASC application form (free). Decision typically within
3 months.

The platform's **Charity Accounts** module works for CASCs too — the chart of
accounts (membership subscriptions, green fees, bar income) maps cleanly to
how HMRC expects CASCs to report.
