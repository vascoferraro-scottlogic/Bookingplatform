Welcome! This help centre is for **club admins** running the day-to-day of your bowling club on the platform.

## What you'll find here

- **Bookings** — accept, reject and refund member bookings.
- **Greens & rinks** — configure your venue layout.
- **Users** — invite, suspend and assign roles to your members.
- **Content** — edit your public landing page (hero, about, photos, contact, map).
- **Events** — create and publish club events; opt in to share with other clubs.
- **Messaging** — channel-based chat for members and committees.
- **Maintenance** — track tasks across the club.
- **Notifications & analytics** — keep an eye on what's happening.

## Getting unstuck

Every dashboard page has a small **?** icon next to the title that links straight to the relevant article in this help centre. Use the search bar at the top of `/dashboard/help` to jump directly to a topic.

:::platform-admin
You're seeing this article in **platform admin context**. When you're [impersonating a tenant](/dashboard/help/users-roles/impersonation), every action you take is recorded against your real identity *and* the tenant. Be especially careful with destructive actions — the club won't necessarily know you made the change unless you tell them.
:::
