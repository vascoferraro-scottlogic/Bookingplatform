Your club's bookable layout is modelled as **Greens** containing **Rinks**. A single booking can span multiple rinks, but every booking slot is tied to exactly one rink at a specific time.

## Adding a green

1. Open **Greens** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add green**, give it a name (e.g. "Main Green") and save.
3. Add rinks to the green — typically four to eight per green.

## Renaming or removing

Renames take effect immediately and are reflected on the public availability grid. Removing a green is blocked if there are any future confirmed bookings against its rinks; cancel or reschedule those first.

## Geolocation (for weather)

Setting latitude and longitude on the club enables the weather forecast on the booking page. If you see the amber banner *"Set your venue location to enable weather forecasts"*, add the coordinates from the **Tenant settings** page.
