The **Analytics** page summarises your club's site traffic and feature usage over the last 7 / 30 / 90 days.

## Summary cards

- **Sessions** — distinct visits, anonymous + signed in.
- **Page views** — total pages loaded.
- **Feature uses** — recorded when a member uses a tracked feature (booking grid, events, messaging, …).
- **PWA share** — proportion of sessions on the installed app vs a regular browser.

## Top pages and features

These rankings help you see where members spend time and which capabilities get the most use. A sudden drop in a top feature is usually the first sign of a regression — worth a closer look in the audit log.

## Privacy

Anonymous visitors are tracked by a daily-rotating SHA-256 fingerprint that contains no personal data. Signed-in visitors are linked to their user id but only for traffic to your own tenant.
